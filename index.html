Straight talk

What YoyliOS is — and what it isn't

It is

  • A complete kids' computer on a USB stick — games, learning, school tools, Robo
  • Fully offline: no accounts, no tracking, no data collection
  • Light enough for a 4 GB RAM PC — the old laptop is exactly the point
  • Backed up to USB: profiles and your kids' work restore in minutes

It isn't

  • Not for Apple Silicon Macs (M1 and newer). Older Intel Macs may work but aren't officially supported
  • Not a subscription — and not a tablet replacement; it needs a real PC and a 16 GB+ USB stick
  • Robo is an AI: helpful, kid-safe by design — and sometimes wrong
  • Not the open internet: if your child needs a browser and YouTube, this is deliberately not that

Needs: a 64-bit PC (most Windows-era laptops & desktops) · 4 GB RAM minimum, 8 GB recommended · 16 GB+ USB stick · no internet required. Changed your mind after installing? The uninstaller removes YoyliOS and restores your computer just as it was.

Questions

Before you ask

Can I get a refund?
Yes. See the refund policy for details.
Does it work on a Mac?
Older Intel-based Macs may work but aren't officially supported. Apple Silicon Macs (M1 and newer) are not supported.
Will it erase my computer?
No. You can run YoyliOS from the USB stick without touching what's on the PC. Installing to the disk is optional, and you choose it.
Do I need internet?
No. Everything, including Robo, works offline. Wi-Fi is optional.
I need help installing.
Step-by-step instructions are included. You can also email us at yoylios.app@gmail.com.
Is it a subscription?
No. You pay $19 once and get a lifetime license key.
What if the PC won't boot from USB?
That's the most common hiccup, and it's a two-minute fix: the included guide walks you through the one BIOS/boot-menu key for your PC brand, step by step. If you get stuck, email us — a human answers.
